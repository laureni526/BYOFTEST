# Story Card

## Story name

Laid off twice, started fixing computers

## What happened

In my mid-20s, I was laid off twice in about six months during the dot-com downturn. I didn't have a plan to become an entrepreneur, but I knew how to fix computers and people needed help, so I started doing IT support on my own. I was often the only woman doing the technical work, and I definitely didn't feel like I had everything figured out before I started. Most of what I learned came after I had already said yes to something and then had to work out how to deliver it.

## What it revealed

This story tells me about the world and how it works by showing that the only person who can hold myself back is me. It was a great lesson in learning not to be perfect, and not to wait for everything to be exactly as I wanted it to be or thought it should be. It created an opportunity for me to think about how I get started and what is needed at a minimum to launch. There is no such thing as perfect. And it's important to remember that everything gets better. When I make mistakes and learn from them, So I build my own business. I didn't know what I was doing, and I did it anyway. There were things that I loved, and there were things that I hated. But some of them were my own choices and, therefore, my own fault. I could have hired people. to help me do the things that I didn't wanna do, and I certainly didn't need to do billing at ten PM at night. Owning my own business taught me that I could take ownership of myself and my actions to support commitments I had made to people, most importantly, myself.
