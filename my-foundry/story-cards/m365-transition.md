# Story Card

## Story name

Teaching the team to experiment through the Microsoft 365 transition

## What happened

When my team moved from Google Workspace into Microsoft 365, I didn't know Office Scripts, Power Automate, VBA, or every feature in Copilot well enough to teach everyone exactly what to do. I taught my team how to use Google Apps Script to drive automations for data analysis using AI to help them write code.

I knew enough to help people get started, and then they began solving their own problems. One built a VBA to automate analysis. Another started combining Office Scripts and Power Automate across a workflow with multiple tabs. The next one went further and used several tools together to build a dashboard that synthesized information from multiple files. I wasn't the technical expert behind each solution. My role became making it normal for them to experiment, bring me something at 25% instead of waiting until it was polished, share what they learned, and keep building from each other's work. This is growing into an ecosystem, where they need to communicate, collaborate, and be inspired by each other in order to build and bring efficiency into our team. The head of our business unit is so excited at how much the team is building to reach coaching details for our operations managers to better support their direct reports. And it only keeps growing.

## What it revealed

I don't have to be the person who has all of the knowledge or makes everything happen. I have to be the person who holds space for others to grow into those opportunities and can bring their own ideas to the table.
