# Story Card

## Story name

Letting people ask instead of deciding for me

## What happened

I've had people on my team tell me they sometimes hesitate to ask for time because they can see how full my calendar is. They'll wait for their next 1:1 or decide their question probably isn't important enough to interrupt me. That bothers me because I don't want them making the decision for me about whether I have capacity. I'd rather they ask and let me set the boundary. Sometimes I can give them 15 minutes right away. Sometimes I'll ask them to send the question in Slack so I can think about it between meetings. Sometimes we really do have to wait. I know I can look unavailable when my calendar is packed, even when my intention is to make my team a priority. I've had to think more deliberately about the difference between being willing to help and making that willingness visible to other people.

## What it revealed

This one seems a bit different. It's still about how I hold space for people, but it's more about how I give people permission to ask, rather than me getting out of their way so they can do big things. I'm not sure if this one is fully connected to the others.
